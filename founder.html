Why I Built Bharat AGI 3026 Worldwide?
Earlier it was for Jharkhand, but now I want it for World. A farmer in Jharkhand speaks Nagpuri, a student in Spain speaks Spanish, a deaf community in Japan speaks JSL. Language should not be a barrier. So I built Bharat AGI 3026 Worldwide - Upload any English document, select any regional or foreign language, and it will CONVERT and SPEAK like a human in that language. No # % reading. Only human sentences. 100% Offline knowledge base, online translation when available, fallback to offline.

This platform is for Hephzibah Home, for every village in Jharkhand, and for every person in the world who needs assistive tech.
