Why I Built Bharat AGI 3026?
Because rural Bharat speaks Nagpuri, Bhojpuri, Magahi - not just English. Deaf-mute communities need gesture to voice in their own language. I wanted to make an AGI that works 100% offline, no internet, privacy first, and speaks like a human - not reading # % symbols.
